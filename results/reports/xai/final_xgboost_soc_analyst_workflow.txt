CURRENT DETECTION WORKFLOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Network-flow data is processed into the 78 model features.
2. XGBoost predicts the network-flow class.
3. Prediction confidence is calculated.
4. SHAP identifies the features contributing to the prediction.
5. The alert engine assigns severity and SOC priority.
6. A human-readable explanation is generated.
7. The recommendation engine creates an investigation recommendation.
8. The alert is placed into the SOC analyst queue.
9. The analyst reviews the evidence and related telemetry.
